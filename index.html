How to Play Google Minesweeper

Game Objective

The goal in Minesweeper is to uncover all safe squares on the field while avoiding mines.

Basic Controls

  • Click to open a square.
  • A number on a square shows how many mines are adjacent to it.
  • Use the numbers to deduce where mines are and uncover safe squares without hitting a mine.
  • If you click on a mine, it's game over.

Tips

  • Right-click to place a flag and mark a mine.
  • Combine number clues to predict mine locations.

What is Minesweeper?

Minesweeper is a classic puzzle game that combines simplicity with strategic depth. The goal is to uncover all safe squares on the board without hitting any hidden mines. Numbers on opened squares indicate how many mines are adjacent, helping you deduce safe spots.

Basic Rules

  • The board contains hidden mines.
  • Click on safe squares to reveal them.
  • Numbers indicate the count of adjacent mines.
  • Use these clues to find all safe squares.
  • Clicking on a mine ends the game.

Why Minesweeper is Fun

Though the rules are simple, Minesweeper tests your logic and attention. With multiple difficulty levels, beginners can start easy and move up to more challenging games. As you learn to predict mine locations, the game becomes even more enjoyable.
